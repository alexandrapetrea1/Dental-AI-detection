The accurate and timely interpretation of dental radiographs is a critical component of modern endodontics and restorative dentistry. However, manual analysis remains a highly subjective, time-consuming process susceptible to human error and fatigue. To address this limitation, this thesis presents the design, development, and validation of an AI-assisted full-stack web platform explicitly engineered for automated anomaly detection in dental X-rays.

The core of the proposed system leverages YOLOv8, a state-of-the-art single-stage object detection architecture, trained on a comprehensively annotated and unified dataset. A significant personal contribution of this work lies in the methodological aggregation and remapping of heterogeneous clinical data to train a model capable of high-precision classification across multiple dental pathologies contextually overlapping, such as caries, lesions, and metallic restorations. Furthermore, the platform integrates this predictive model into a lightweight, real-time React and FastAPI ecosystem, delivering a seamless diagnostic dashboard that highlights anomalies directly to the practitioner.

The structure of this thesis is divided into six chapters. Chapter 1 introduces the medical context and the objectives of the research. Chapter 2 provides a comprehensive literature review of the current computer vision paradigms in medical imaging. Chapter 3 establishes the theoretical framework, detailing convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and the YOLO architecture. Chapter 4 documents the core original contributions, including the software architecture, the dataset curation process, and the full-stack implementation of the web platform. Chapter 5 focuses on experimental results, evaluating the AI model's precision and structural performance during inference. Finally, Chapter 6 concludes the research by summarizing the achievements and outlining potential avenues for future technological enhancements in automated dentistry.
